Melyvio is a free, open-source music player for Android that plays music from YouTube Music. You never have to log in, there are no ads and no tracking.

Main features

- Music DNA: your top artists, songs, genres and listening time, plus a music personality, worked out on your phone and shareable as an image
- Audio booster: add up to 100% extra loudness, with a safe range shown
- Crossfade: fade between songs, 3, 5 or 8 seconds
- Playlist backup: export a playlist and import it again after a reinstall
- Custom playlist banners and pinned playlists
- Synced lyrics, equalizer, sleep timer, offline downloads
- Music recognition, Android Auto and widgets
- Material 3 design with dynamic colours and a glass bottom bar

Your listening history stays on your phone, and you can pause or clear it any time.

Melyvio is built on Metrolist. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
