Privacy Policy

This app is committed to protecting your privacy and does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No Registration or Accounts: The app operates entirely without user accounts. You do not need to log in, register, or provide any personal details (such as your name or email address) to use the music player.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not track your listening habits, search history, or app interactions. No analytics tools, trackers, or cookies are integrated into the application.
  • No Advertisements: This application is completely ad-free and does not communicate with third-party advertising networks.
  • Third-Party Services (YouTube Music): As a third-party client, the app fetches public audio stream and metadata content directly from YouTube Music to play music. This application does not transmit any of your personal data to YouTube Music. However, your interactions with YouTube Music’s public servers are subject to YouTube's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Local Data Only: Any app configurations, such as your locally created playlists or favorites, are stored strictly on your own device and are never uploaded to any external servers.
