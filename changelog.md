# Changelog

Big changes to Melyvio are listed here. The first news about each version is posted on the Telegram channel before the release goes up: https://t.me/rawxtremestudios

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Music DNA: top artists, songs, genres, listening time, weekly chart, liked tracks, newly found artists, and a music personality. Worked out on the phone. Shareable as an image
- Audio booster: up to 100% extra loudness, with the phone volume shown first and a safe range marked
- Quick crossfade control in the player menu (3, 5 or 8 seconds)
- Playlist backup: export a playlist and import it into a new playlist later, with a loading screen and a list of any songs that could not be added
- Custom playlist banners and pinned playlists
- New home screen: greeting with your own name and photo, banner slides with Play Now, mood chips, Speed dial pages
- Glass bottom bar that shrinks while scrolling, with the playing song docked into it
- New glass player controls
- No login. Your library and history stay on the phone
- Update check from GitHub releases, with notifications
